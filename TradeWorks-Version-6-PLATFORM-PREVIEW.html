Why TradeWorks Exists

Career decisions should come with a test drive.

TradeWorks is designed to show students and career changers that college, trade school, apprenticeship and employer training are all possible routes. The goal is not to tell someone which path to choose—it is to let them explore before investing years of time and money.

1. Discover

Learn what each trade does, how people train, and where the career can lead.

2. Experience

Work through simplified job situations and choose what you would do next.

3. Test Yourself

Take knowledge checks with explanations, not just right/wrong scores.

4. Find Your Fit

Compare your interests with different skilled-trade environments.

Ways to Get Started

There is more than one training path

Apprenticeship

Technical instruction plus supervised paid work.

Trade / Technical School

Focused hands-on training for entry-level skill and certifications.

Employer Training

Some employers hire entry-level workers and train them on the job.

Community College

Certificates and associate programs can combine technical and general education.
